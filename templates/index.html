Decision logging + analysis system

Audit decisions with explainable AI support.

PDAS helps users log decisions, detect risky patterns, and review audit-ready explanations. It is a decision-support tool, not a decision-maker, chatbot, prediction engine, or diagnosis tool.

Create Account Log In

Core Modules

  • User Module: login, add decisions, view history
  • Decision Analysis Engine: TF-IDF similarity, rule-based scoring, pattern detection
  • Audit Report Generator: risk summary, detected patterns, suggestions
  • Admin Module: configure rule notes and view analytics

Risk score logic

  • High stress (>4) → +2 risk
  • Low confidence (≤2) → +2 risk
  • Repeated similar decisions → +2 risk
  • Negative past outcome → +3 risk

Risk levels

  • 0–3 → Low Risk
  • 4–6 → Medium Risk
  • 7+ → High Risk

Included AI techniques

  • NLP with TF-IDF + cosine similarity
  • Rule-based risk scoring
  • Pattern detection across historical decisions
